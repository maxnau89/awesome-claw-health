# Awesome Claw Health

A curated list of high-signal projects, experiments, skills, agents, and research workflows using **OpenClaw-inspired** and adjacent technology for **personal health**, **quantified self**, **wearables**, **bioinformatics**, and **health/biomedical research**.

This list is intentionally broad around:

- `OpenClaw` and OpenClaw-native skills
- `Claude Code`, coding agents, and agentic developer tooling
- agent skills, tool-using assistants, and local-first health copilots
- biomedical research agents, literature agents, and bioinformatics workflows

## Status Markers

- `🦖` OpenClaw-native or strongly OpenClaw-inspired
- `⌚` Wearables, quantified-self, or on-device health tracking
- `🧪` Biomedical research, bioinformatics, or literature work
- `🛠️` Developer workflow, observability, or companion tooling
- `💵` Paid service or paid tier commonly required

## Personal Health and Quantified Self

- [the-momentum/open-wearables](https://github.com/the-momentum/open-wearables) `⌚` - Self-hosted platform to unify wearable health data through one AI-ready API.
- [mrmujo/health_assistant](https://github.com/mrmujo/health_assistant) `⌚` - Personal health assistant centered on wearable data, health logging, and AI-assisted analysis.
- [huifer/WellAlly-health](https://github.com/huifer/WellAlly-health) `⌚` - Claude-based healthcare assistant for symptom tracking, medication management, and medical-record workflows.

## Wearables and On-Device Agents

- [OwlAIProject/Owl](https://github.com/OwlAIProject/Owl) `⌚` - Personal wearable AI that runs locally.
- [ThinkOffApp/ClawWatch](https://github.com/ThinkOffApp/ClawWatch) `⌚` - Smartwatch-native AI agent combining offline speech recognition with Claude on Galaxy Watch.
- [majidmanzarpour/vibe-check](https://github.com/majidmanzarpour/vibe-check) `⌚🛠️` - Claude Code companion plugin for stretch reminders, eye breaks, and hydration nudges while coding.

## OpenClaw-Native Bio and Health Projects

- [ClawBio/ClawBio](https://github.com/ClawBio/ClawBio) `🦖🧪` - Bioinformatics-native AI agent skill library built on OpenClaw with a local-first and reproducible workflow focus.

## Biomedical Research and Bioinformatics Agents

- [BioDSA](https://biodsa.github.io/) `🧪` - Open-source framework for prototyping, reproducing, and benchmarking biomedical data science agents.
- [biagent-dev/bia](https://github.com/biagent-dev/bia) `🧪` - BioInformatics Agent focused on reshaping bioinformatics workflows with LLM-driven agents.
- [bobqwera/biomedagent](https://github.com/bobqwera/biomedagent) `🧪` - Multi-agent biomedical analysis project for natural-language-driven bioinformatics work.
- [mewz1k/pubmed_agent](https://github.com/mewz1k/pubmed_agent) `🧪` - Lightweight agent for interacting with PubMed.
- [Citrus086/medical-rag-agent](https://github.com/Citrus086/medical-rag-agent) `🧪` - Medical RAG and multi-agent knowledge retrieval workflow for health information tasks.

## What Belongs Here

Projects are a good fit if they use agentic or assistant-style technology for one or more of the following:

- personal health tracking, symptom journaling, medication workflows, or habit support
- wearables, health-device integrations, or quantified-self infrastructure
- biomedical literature search, PubMed workflows, trial screening, or evidence synthesis
- bioinformatics, computational biology, genomics, or reproducible research automation
- OpenClaw skills, Claude Code workflows, coding agents, or agent platforms applied to health
